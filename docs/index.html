Everything You Need

Professional-grade forensic capabilities packed into a single self-contained executable inside a standard installer.

🔎

Device Identification

Stage one: make, model, serial, Android version, build fingerprint, storage and installed packages, read over ADB and recorded against the case before anything is acquired.

📥

Data Acquisition

Acquisition pipeline: SMS, calls, contacts, social media, deleted files, documents, emails, browser data. Imaging output is selectable - Raw (dd) segments or ZIP - with per-image hashing and verification, and progress in real percentages with a rate and an ETA on the forty-minute logical capture.

💳

SIM Info

Reads the UICC through the handset: every slot and its state, carrier and network code, ICCID, IMSI and MSISDN per subscription, with the subscriber number pulled from three sources when the platform will part with it. Android's redaction of identifier tails is stated in the record, not padded over, and a page-level note says what the handset cannot give up.

🔍

Examination & Analysis

Run 11 analysis modules over the preserved copy: call logs, SMS/MMS, contacts, browser history, GPS, WiFi, Bluetooth, app artifacts, calendar, deleted data and notes. Read-only — it parses from the acquired copy, never the device.

📄

Court-Ready Reporting

Generate forensic reports in HTML, PDF, JSON, or an XLSX verification workbook that independently re-hashes every artifact. Written in plain English for lawyers and judges.

💻

Zero Installation

Single self-contained .exe wrapped in a standard setup wizard. ADB drivers bundled inside. No Python, no dependencies. Run the installer, or double-click the portable exe.

🌙

Dark, Light, or System Theme

The interface opens dark and switches from the View menu — light, or follow the operating system as it changes. Your choice is remembered between launches. Exported reports keep their own fixed palette, so a court document never changes appearance because a UI preference did.

📷

Thumbnail Grid & Screen Capture

Media categories open as a Cellebrite-style thumbnail grid at three tile sizes, pulled in the background and cached for display only. Capture Screen takes a PNG of whatever is on the handset over adb exec-out screencap, hashes it, and files it as a finding — the way to preserve a conversation the tool cannot read natively.

📊

Grid or Table, Your Call

The curated table stays the default, because a table is what an examiner cites. One toggle re-projects the same rows as a card grid — category accent, summary, timestamp and count on every tile — for the browse-first pass through a large artifact set. The grid caps itself at 400 cards and says so, instead of quietly rendering a view that would take minutes to lay out.

⚡

Responsive Under Load

Every ADB call runs off the GUI thread, so the window keeps drawing while the handset answers. Device scans are asynchronous and poll, acquisition owns the channel while it runs, and the artifact viewer defers its own crawl rather than fighting for it. Counting is single-pass, persistence is batched — a seventy-thousand-artifact phone reads in minutes without a frozen frame.

🔌

Waits for a Ready Handset

An unauthorised or offline device is reported as exactly that, not as Connected. The viewer refuses to crawl a handset that has not answered the authorisation prompt, polls until state is device, and retries the load — so plugging in a locked phone produces a status line instead of a blank panel and a silent two-minute timeout.

💬

WhatsApp Without Root

Chat exports, a plain msgstore.db, crypt15 inventory for the encrypted store, and notification previews — with WhatsApp's own "this message was deleted" markers and .trashed-* recovery kept as findings. What the handset will not expose is listed as unreachable instead of reported as zero.

✈️

Telegram, Read Honestly

Chats, groups, channels and media from a Telegram export or a readable cache, plus notifications captured on the device. Four artifact view modes — table, timeline, thread and raw — let the same evidence be read the way the question needs it.

🖥️

Physical Image on a Rooted Handset

The partition map is captured first, then boot and recovery are imaged over dd, pulled, hashed and filed as physical-image artifacts. On an unrooted handset the phase says the device refused root instead of quietly doing nothing — and the report states the honest ceiling, which is ciphertext for userdata.

📝

Cases You Can Correct

A mistyped case number, title, investigator or status is fixable from Case Management, and a case opened by mistake can be deleted — with a separate question about whether the evidence folder goes with it. Folders are left alone unless you say otherwise.

🏷️

Its Own Mark

The executable carries the DroidForenX emblem in the taskbar and in Explorer, and the sidebar carries the wordmark. The sidebar also folds away with Ctrl+B when a table needs the width, and a grip stays on screen to bring it back.
